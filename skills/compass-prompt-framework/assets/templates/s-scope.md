# Scope (S) template

Scope is mandatory. It governs the work while it is under way; Success spec governs the finished artifact. Phrase rules positively where possible: tell the model what to do rather than only what to avoid.

```xml
<scope>
- In scope: [what the work covers]
- Out of scope / non-goals: [what it must not do or decide]
- Prohibited shortcuts: [for example retries, skips, invented figures, unstated promises]
- Limits: [length, time, run, or budget limits, and what to do when one is reached]
- Assumption policy: [ask before proceeding | proceed and label every assumption]
</scope>
```

Checklist:

- [ ] Boundaries: what is in and out, and the non-goals.
- [ ] Prohibited shortcuts that would satisfy the letter of the Mission but defeat its intention.
- [ ] Length, budget, or stopping limits, with the action to take at the limit.
- [ ] An explicit assumption policy.
- [ ] Rules phrased as actions where possible ("Use only the facts above") rather than only prohibitions.
